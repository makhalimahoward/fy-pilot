Supervision that keeps every project on track

One shared record for allocations, sessions, milestones and progress, so students, supervisors and the coordinator always know what's next.

Why FYPilot exists

Final year supervision at NUST still runs on emails, WhatsApp chats and handwritten notes. Nobody has one reliable view of who is on track, so problems only show up after a deadline has passed.

One shared record

Every session, feedback item and deadline lives in one place, so nothing depends on memory or a phone search.

Early warnings

Overdue milestones are flagged as they happen, so students and supervisors can act before the deadline passes.

Time for supervision

Students see what is next and supervisors see who needs them, so meeting time goes further.

How it works

Four steps from project allocation to final submission.

  1. Allocate

    Coordinator assigns students and projects

  2. Supervise

    Sessions, feedback and action items

  3. Track

    Milestones and deadlines, flagged early

  4. Submit

    Documents reviewed and approved

Built for every role

Each user sees only what their role needs.

Students

Track milestones, read feedback, upload documents and see your next deadline.

Supervisors

Manage your students in one list, log sessions and review submissions.

Coordinator

See every project at a glance, flag who is falling behind and export reports.
