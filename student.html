Web Based Final Year Project Supervision and Progress Monitoring System

A shared record for allocations, supervision sessions, milestones and documents, so students, supervisors and the coordinator work from the same information.

Objectives

  1. 1Allocate each student to a supervisor and a project, with the details recorded in one place.
  2. 2Record every supervision session, feedback note and action item against the student.
  3. 3Track milestones and deadlines, and flag anything overdue as it happens.
  4. 4Give the coordinator a view of progress across all projects, with reports they can export.

In scope

  • Allocations and project details
  • Sessions, feedback and action items
  • Milestones, deadlines and documents
  • Coordinator reports, PDF and CSV

Out of scope

  • Grading
  • Video calls
